SOP-101: Machine Startup Procedure (Packaging Line A)

1. Check that the emergency stop button is released.
2. Verify the air pressure gauge reads between 5 and 7 bar.
3. Turn the main power switch to ON.
4. Wait 30 seconds for the control panel to finish booting.
5. Press the green START button to begin the warm-up cycle.
6. Warm-up takes 10 minutes. Do not load material until the amber light turns off.

If the air pressure is below 5 bar, do not start the machine. Contact the maintenance supervisor, Ravi Menon.
